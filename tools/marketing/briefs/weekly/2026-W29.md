## Quora (сб W9: 2 ответа)

**Q: How should I analyze my chess games without a strong engine?**
A: Write your own notes first: the 2-3 moments you felt lost, and every move you already
suspect was bad — plus WHY you played it (missed idea? lazy calculation? time trouble?).
Then check with any engine, even a weak one. The gap between your notes and the verdict
tells you whether your evaluation or your discipline failed — those need different fixes.
After ~20 games your recurring-mistakes list writes itself, and that list IS your study plan.

**Q: How often should I do chess puzzles to actually improve?**
A: Daily beats long weekend sessions: 10–15 minutes every day builds pattern recognition
better than 2 hours once a week. Two upgrades: solve from positions that resemble YOUR
games (mistake-based training), and after a failed puzzle, don't reveal the answer —
retry it tomorrow. Spaced repetition works for tactics exactly like for vocabulary.
